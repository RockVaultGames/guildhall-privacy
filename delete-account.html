Delete Your Account — Guild Hall: Idle RPG

Guild Hall: Idle RPG is developed by RockVault Games. You can delete your account and all data stored under it at any time, in either of two ways.

In the app

  1. Open Guild Hall and go to the Guild tab.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Under Danger, tap Delete account and confirm.

The account is deleted immediately. You need to be online.

By request

If you no longer have the app installed, email rockvaultgames@gmail.com from the email address linked to your account, with the subject "Delete my Guild Hall account". We will delete the account within 30 days and confirm by reply. An account with no linked email can only be deleted from inside the app, since we have no other way to know it is yours.

What is deleted

Your account (the player ID and any linked email address), your guild and all game progress, your leaderboard entries, your gameplay events and app-open records, and our record of your purchases. Nothing is kept on our servers.

What is kept

RevenueCat, Google Play and the Apple App Store keep their own records of purchase transactions, as they are required to for refunds, receipts and tax. These are not controlled by us.
