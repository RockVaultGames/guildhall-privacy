Privacy Policy — Guild Hall: Idle RPG

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Guild Hall: Idle RPG ("the app") is developed by RockVault Games ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have. In short: the app gives you an anonymous account so your guild is saved to our servers; you can link an email address if you want to move your guild to another phone; we record gameplay events to improve the game; the app contains no ads and no advertising trackers; and we never sell your data.

Your account and your save

When you start a guild, the app creates an anonymous account for you: a random player ID with no name or email attached. Your game progress (your guild, its members, gear, currencies and purchase grants) is stored under that ID on our servers, hosted by Supabase, so the game can check results and keep your save safe.

You can choose to link an email address. We then send a one-time sign-in code to that address, and you can use it to load your guild on another phone. We use your email address only to operate your account; we do not send marketing email.

Gameplay analytics

The app records gameplay events on our own servers: for example, that a guild was started, which tutorial steps and fights were completed and whether they were won, levels reached, recruits signed, when the store was opened, and when the app was opened (with the app version and whether the phone is iOS or Android). These events are linked to your player ID. We use them only to understand where players get stuck and to improve the game. We do not use any third-party analytics service.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see or store your payment card details. Purchases are validated through RevenueCat, a purchase-processing service, which tells our servers what was bought so the app can deliver it once. We keep a record of completed purchases with your account so that what you bought can be restored.

Advertising and tracking

The app contains no ads and no advertising SDK. We do not collect an advertising ID, we do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not share data with advertisers or data brokers.

Notifications

With your permission, the app shows reminders (for example, when a party has returned). These are scheduled on your device; no notification data is sent to us.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • You can play without ever giving an email address.
  • You can delete your account and everything stored under it from inside the app (Settings › Danger › Delete account) or by request — see Delete Your Account.

Children's privacy

Guild Hall: Idle RPG is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has linked an email address, contact us and we will delete the account.
